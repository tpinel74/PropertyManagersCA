How It Works

Three steps to unlocking resident benefits

Share this simple journey with your tenants. It costs them nothing.

01

Check eligibility

Residents of CA-assessed properties automatically qualify. If they live in Columbia, they're almost certainly in.

02

Get a free Columbia Card

Residents register online or through CA Customer Care. All they need is a Columbia address and proof of residency.

03

Enjoy resident-rate perks

Pools, fitness clubs, tennis, golf, programs, and more -- all at meaningfully lower resident pricing.

The Columbia Card

Free for every Columbia resident

The Columbia Card is the credential that unlocks resident pricing across all CA facilities and programs.

Free
  • Resident-rate pricing on all CA memberships and programs
  • 4 complimentary pool day passes per family each summer
  • Free entry every 2nd Sunday, June through August
  • Simple online application -- just a Columbia address and proof of residency
