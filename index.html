How It Works

Three steps to unlocking resident benefits

Share this simple journey with your tenants — it costs them nothing, and gives them a lot.

01

Find out if you're eligible

Residents of CA-assessed properties — including apartment buildings in Columbia — automatically qualify for CA resident benefits. Most renters don't realize this. If they live here, they're in.

02

Get your free Columbia Card

Residents register for their Columbia Card online or through CA Customer Care. They'll need a Columbia address and a simple proof of residency (lease or utility bill). It's free — always.

03

Enjoy resident-rate perks

With a Columbia Card, residents unlock deeply discounted membership rates, free pool day passes, 2nd Sunday Swim access, and resident pricing across all CA programs and facilities.

What Residents Get

A community built for living well

CA resident benefits are an amenity your building is already delivering. Help your tenants discover what's available to them.

🏊

Pools — 23 outdoor + indoor facilities

Resident-rate access to CA's network of pools across Columbia. Columbia Cardholders also receive 4 complimentary single-use guest passes each summer season.

💪

Fitness Clubs — 3 full-service locations

Resident rates on gym memberships (1Fit and Fit&Play) at Columbia Athletic Club, Columbia Gym, and Supreme Sports Club — significantly lower than non-resident pricing.

🎾

Tennis & Pickleball

Resident discounts at CA's 5 tennis clubs. The Play membership (pools + tennis) is just $32/month for CA residents — compared to $67 for non-residents.

⛳

Golf — 2 courses

Resident rates on golf memberships at Hobbit's Glen and other CA golf facilities. A significant saving for golf-loving residents compared to non-resident rates.

🧒

Family & Youth Programs

KidSpace childcare, before & after school care, summer camps, youth & teen center, arts programs, and swim lessons — all at resident pricing. Income-qualified households receive an additional 50% discount.

🌳

Open Space & Pathways

Free access to nearly 3,600 acres of open space, 95 miles of walking and biking pathways, lakes, parks, dog parks, and tot lots throughout Columbia.

🎉

Community Events

Free entertainment, multicultural events, Lakefront Live concerts, wellness programming, and more throughout the year — open to all Columbia residents.

🏒

Ice Skating, Art Center & More

Resident rates at the Columbia Ice Rink, Columbia Art Center, Columbia Horse Center, Sports Park, and Roller Skate Arena — an unmatched range of amenities.

A benefit you may not know you have — and it's free.

Living at [BUILDING NAME] means you're part of something bigger. As a resident of a CA-assessed property in Columbia, Maryland, you're entitled to Columbia Association resident benefits — the same perks enjoyed by Columbia homeowners.

Columbia Association (CA) is the nonprofit community services organization behind Columbia's pools, fitness clubs, open spaces, youth programs, and much more. Residents of CA-assessed properties — including renters — receive significant discounts on memberships and programs across all of CA's facilities.

Here's what your CA residency unlocks:
• Resident-rate memberships — pools + tennis from $32/mo; gym from $70/mo; family packages from $46/mo
• 4 complimentary pool day passes per family each summer (with Columbia Card)
• Free entry every 2nd Sunday of the month, June through August (2nd Sunday Swim)
• Discounts on group fitness classes, youth programs, golf, ice skating, and more
• 50% discount for income-qualified households
• Free access to 3,600 acres of open space and 95 miles of pathways

Getting started takes just a few minutes. Request your free Columbia Card at columbiaassociation.org/columbia-card or contact CA Customer Care at 410-730-1801 (Mon–Fri, 9am–4pm).
