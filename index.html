Columbia Association — Partner Resources

Help the people you serve discover what Columbia offers.

Columbia Association manages the pools, parks, fitness clubs, open space, and community programs that define life in Columbia, Maryland. This resource is for the professionals who work with Columbia residents -- helping them understand and access the CA benefits they're already entitled to.

Columbia Association (CA) is the nonprofit community services organization behind Columbia's 43+ facilities, 23 outdoor pools, 95 miles of pathways, and the full range of programs and amenities that make Columbia one of the best-planned communities in America. Residents of CA-assessed properties receive meaningful discounts on memberships and programs through the free Columbia Card.
