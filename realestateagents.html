In the Field

Talking points for buyers and sellers

CA benefits show up differently depending on where your client is in the transaction. Here's how to use them.

For buyers

This address comes with built-in amenities

Buyers are comparing Columbia properties to alternatives across the region. CA's network -- 23 pools, three fitness clubs, 95 miles of trails, and more -- is a genuine differentiator that's easy to quantify. A family Play membership for a CA resident runs $46/month versus $86/month for a non-resident.

For sellers

CA is part of what you're selling

Properties on CA-assessed land carry a community benefit that non-CA properties simply don't. The Columbia Card is free, the amenities are extensive, and most prospective buyers -- especially those coming from outside Columbia -- won't know this without you telling them.

For relocation clients

Columbia is intentionally designed for this

Columbia was master-planned from the start around community infrastructure. CA's role isn't incidental -- it's the mechanism that maintains and programs the amenities that come with living here. For clients relocating from other markets, this context matters.

The Columbia Card

What your clients get -- and how they get it

When a buyer asks how this actually works, here's the answer. The Columbia Card is free, simple, and unlocks resident pricing across all CA facilities.

01

Confirm eligibility

Residents of CA-assessed properties qualify automatically. If the property is in Columbia, it almost certainly qualifies -- use the address lookup to confirm.

02

Register online

New residents apply at columbiaassociation.org or through CA Customer Care. They'll need a Columbia address and basic proof of residency -- typically a lease or utility bill.

03

Unlock resident rates

The Columbia Card is free, always. With it, residents receive discounted membership rates, 4 complimentary pool day passes each summer, and free 2nd Sunday Swim access June through August.
