Property Managers

Your residents have benefits they may not know about.

Every resident living on CA-assessed property - including renters - is entitled to Columbia Association resident benefits, including discounted memberships and reduced admissions rates to pools, fitness programming, tennis, golf, ice skating, art classes and so much more. This page gives you what you need to let them know.

Columbia Association (CA) is the nonprofit behind Columbia's 43+ facilities, 23 outdoor pools, 95 miles of pathways, and the full range of community programs that define life here. Residents of CA-assessed properties receive meaningful discounts on memberships through the free Columbia Card -- a tangible benefit that comes with the address.

The Columbia Card

Free for every Columbia resident

The Columbia Card is the credential that unlocks resident pricing across all CA facilities and programs.

Free
  • Resident-rate pricing on all CA memberships and programs
  • 4 complimentary pool day passes per family each summer
  • Free pool entry every 2nd Sunday, June through August
  • Simple application - confirm your Columbia address and provide proof of residency
